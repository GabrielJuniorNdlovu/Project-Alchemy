## 1.9 Work Plan

The working schedule spans 16 weeks from a start date that remains to be confirmed. The chart is a proposed allocation, not evidence that procurement or institutional review can be completed within the first period. Recording activities must move if their prerequisites are delayed.

| Documentation and dissertation preparation | ■ | ■ | ■ | ■ | Record actual results, failures and incomplete work |


This Gantt-style allocation is contingent. Dataset size and session spacing must follow the approved protocol, not be compressed merely to fit the chart. Final test data must remain separate even if recording is delayed. Training and final evaluation cannot be completed without sufficient personal recordings.

At each milestone, the researcher will record evidence obtained and remaining blockers. Where access, approvals, data quality or integration prevent completion, the work plan will identify the required resource or calendar change. It will not redefine an offline demonstration as completion of the live system.

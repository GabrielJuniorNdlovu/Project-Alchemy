# Chapter 1 literature matrix

**Research log:** Search performed 28 October 2026. The workspace index is a discovery list, not proof. Web searches used combinations of “motor imagery calibration free online detection”, “cross subject transfer learning”, “cross session EEG dataset”, “asynchronous non control false positives”, and “EEG webcam eye tracking”; primary records were sought on PubMed, publisher sites, PMC, PhysioNet and BNCI. SciSpace search: “How do EEG motor imagery brain computer interfaces reduce calibration for new users while detecting non-control periods, and what are the experimental limitations?” Consensus returned a monthly quota error. Zotero status returned operation not permitted for the local API; library access was not established. These searches are exploratory, not a systematic review or exhaustive novelty search. Counts of screened search results were not recorded.

**Status terms:** V = title, first author and year checked against a named primary publisher or PubMed record; F = relevant full-text portions inspected; A = abstract or indexed record only. V does not establish every claim or rule out later corrections. NR = not reliably established in the materials inspected. All reported results belong to the cited study's protocol; do not compare them as if measured on the same data.

# Comparative question and evidence limits

Authority: ../goal.md. Current scope: subject-specific live MI-BCI; 2 November 2026.

The project asks whether a lightweight acceptance mechanism offers a useful error–availability trade-off against established rejection methods on personal recordings across sessions and under controlled unfamiliar-task and channel-degradation conditions. It also examines embedded and live application behaviour.

The source matrix records inspected evidence for session variability and false-positive rejection. These findings establish that the underlying problems and rejection approaches already exist. Collecting personal data and deploying on Raspberry Pi do not by themselves establish methodological novelty.

The proposed contribution is a bounded, reproducible engineering investigation. Its value depends on suitable comparisons, independent session evaluation, transparent data provenance and measured live behaviour. A single participant cannot justify a population-level claim.

## Closest-method review still needed

The earlier proposal names Haridharan, Mulder, Xing and Nagarajan, and Liu/TempDens. Their exact metadata, publication status and complete methods require fresh verification before attributing findings or choosing them as baselines. Search/landing-page results in this migration did not provide adequate original support for those claimed references. They are unresolved candidates, not verified evidence of a gap.

Inspect whether each candidate already evaluates learned gates, conformal rejection, feature novelty, session shift, unfamiliar classes and equivalent acceptance. If overlap is substantial, frame the contribution as a replication/engineering evaluation or revise the scientific question with the supervisor. Do not manufacture a gap.

## Interpretation boundaries

Rejection on familiar errors, unfamiliar acceptance and simulated corruption must be separate outcomes. Offline performance does not establish live reliability; runtime measurements do not establish adequate human control; a low accepted-error fraction at near-zero acceptance does not establish usefulness. Report these limitations even if they weaken the proposed hypothesis.

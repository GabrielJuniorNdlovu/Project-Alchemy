## Implication for proposed benchmark

The five-category TUAR subset supports testing a subject-disjoint classifier comparison if data access and annotation structure permit it. It cannot answer whether models generalize to wearable devices. A common split and compute measurement improve interpretability; they do not by themselves guarantee novelty. The two 2026 studies substantially narrow any novelty claim. A supervisor must approve a benchmark-style contribution or the research question must change.
